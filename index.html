LevelPlot Uploader

The publishing tool of LevelPlot, a Russian-language channel of short documentary videos about the history of the video game industry.

What it does

LevelPlot Uploader is a small command-line tool run by the channel owner on their own computer. After the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), it:

  • uploads finished videos produced by the channel to the owner's own YouTube channel (videos.insert);
  • sets the video title, description, tags, category, language and publishing time;
  • sets the custom thumbnail made for the video (thumbnails.set).

The tool is not a public service: it has no other users, it never accesses anyone else's channel, and it does not read, collect or analyse data of YouTube viewers.

YouTube API Services

LevelPlot Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How data is handled is described in our Privacy Policy, which refers to the Google Privacy Policy.
